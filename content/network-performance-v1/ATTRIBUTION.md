## What this package includes

The `upstream` directory preserves the source section file, license, and README exactly as returned for the fixed revision. The full performance source file is retained for provenance; only section 1.5.1 is assigned to the tutor. The original excerpt in `reading-original.rst` preserves lines 14 through 187 without rewriting. Passage records preserve exact line spans and hashes.

The readable `reading.md` view reflows paragraphs and renders source notation. It adds headings and passage labels, omits figure images and their captions, and marks those omissions. Two separately labeled project notes identify a source simplification and a units typo. No textbook illustrations are distributed in this package. Original passage wording is retained; the project notes are not attributed to the textbook authors. The authored concepts, questions, rubrics and demonstration scenarios are stored separately and are not represented as statements or exercises written by the textbook authors.

## Upstream wording discrepancy

The preserved LICENSE grants rights to produce, reproduce and share adapted material subject to its conditions. The preserved README also contains a sentence saying that derivative-work rights are not automatically granted. Those statements conflict. This package retains both notices and records the discrepancy; it does not claim to resolve it or to have obtained additional permission. Keeping an unchanged excerpt and separate authored questions does not itself settle the discrepancy. Clarification from the rights holder remains appropriate if the team needs assurance about a broader reuse.
