## Ranked remaining differences

### P2 — AE-V1: assignment discovery omits the prototype's participation summary

The prototype's class rows show **students who have answered / enrolled students and total answers**, together with activation status. The application rows show enrolled-student counts and active status (and an activation date at the wider layout), but no answered-student/answer-count summary in the corresponding visible row. The difference persists across desktop and mobile; it is a content/representation gap, not an effect of the number of classes or the UUID-heavy fixture names. Counts may be available in Progress, but that is a different navigation step. This is a remaining prototype-parity issue, not evidence that saved responses disappeared.

### P2 — AE-V2: original-response Context has a different comparison structure

At 1440 the prototype Context begins with a compact chronological chain of this answer, follow-up, hint, reading and fresh check, then reading passages. The app Context begins with approved source/version and passage text, followed by assistance and follow-up/counterevidence links. Actual subsequent responses and displayed assistance exist in this fixture, but the visible comparison is presented as separate sections/links rather than the prototype's ordered chain. This reduces the same-screen temporal overview even though original/source/help information remains accessible. It is a bounded visual/capability deviation requiring reviewer disposition, not proof of a lost history or incorrect chronology.

The prototype's highlighted evaluation quote is also absent from this app screenshot. Because this application response has an explicitly synthetic evaluator result and different quote content, this review does not infer a general quote-highlighting defect from that single pair.

### P3 — AE-V3: All answers rows and pane vocabulary diverge from the prototype

Application list cards emphasize original-response excerpts and textual correctness, producing taller rows than the prototype's compact student/step/time/status rows. The app also uses Responses / Record where the prototype uses Answers / Details and reverses the order of mode-tabs versus pane-tabs on phones. At native 200%, the application initial list surface reaches only its Saved responses heading while the prototype reaches the first record. These are lower-priority density/consistency differences; both retain clear selected-state treatment and responsive pane choices.

## Findings deliberately not asserted

- No broken Concern return is inferred from a direct All answers URL without return context.
- No missing lesson lifecycle is inferred from a different mixture of fixtures or a private newer draft above an immutable assigned version.
- No runtime correctness, ownership, navigation or side-effect claim is inferred from a screenshot. The capture report lists no interaction probes for these three states.
- No exact pixel parity is claimed: classes, text lengths, selected record, concept count, source material and lifecycle state are intentionally different.
- The prior composition audit shows the Evidence production scope unchanged from the tested baseline. These are current candidate-versus-prototype observations; they are not attributed to the six-source merge as newly introduced regressions.

The final handoff should retain these discrepancies and the unmatched-entry-state limitation alongside the separate connected lifecycle and navigation results. Independent acceptance remains outstanding.
