CONCERN · TRANSMISSION DELAY

Transmission delay attributed to cable length

Claims transmission delay increases with cable length when packet size and link transmission rate remain fixed.

Be careful: A wrong number, a generic “it takes longer”, or a packet-size statement alone is not evidence for this hypothesis.
