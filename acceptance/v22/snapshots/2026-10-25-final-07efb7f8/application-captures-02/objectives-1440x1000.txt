Evaporation changes liquid water into water vapor, a gas. A puddle can shrink as water evaporates into the air. The water has changed state; it has not disappeared.

Condensation changes water vapor into liquid water. When water vapor in air cools near a cold surface, liquid droplets can form on the surface. Evaporation and condensation describe opposite changes of state.
